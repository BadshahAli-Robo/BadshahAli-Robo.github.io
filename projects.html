Robotic Study Companion (RSC)

Thesis-related contribution under supervisor guidance.

As part of my thesis work under the supervision of Matevž Zorec and Farnaz Baksh, I contributed to the documentation of RSC v4 / v4.1 by creating short assembly videos and supporting the presentation of build steps.

My contribution focused on documentation only. The platform design and development were led by my supervisors.

Pia Prototype Exploration

Supervised prototype exercise based on an open-source robot design.

As part of my thesis-related supervised work, I explored a prototype direction based on the open-source Pia the Robot design. My role focused on hands-on learning, basic prototyping, and exploring form factors for portability under supervisor guidance.

This work was based on the original open-source Pia design by pohukai (Printables model #190775), and all CAD files and design references were taken from that source. This should be understood as a supervised student prototype exercise rather than a completed standalone platform.
