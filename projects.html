Robotic Study Companion (RSC)

Thesis-related documentation contribution under supervisor guidance.

As part of my thesis work under the supervision of Matevž Zorec and Farnaz Baksh, I contributed to the documentation of RSC v4 / v4.1 by creating short assembly videos and supporting the presentation of build steps for the documentation page.

My contribution focused on documenting and communicating the assembly process clearly. The platform design, development, and supervision were led by my supervisors.

Pia Prototype Exploration

Supervised prototype exercise based on an open-source robot design.

As part of my thesis-related supervised work, I explored a prototype direction based on the open-source Pia the Robot design. My role focused on hands-on learning, basic prototyping, and exploring form factors for portability under supervisor guidance.

This work was based on the original open-source Pia design by pohukai (Printables model #190775), and the CAD files and design references were taken from that source. This should be understood as a supervised student prototype exercise rather than a completed standalone platform.

  • Supervised prototyping
  • Form-factor exploration
  • Open-source design
  • Student project

What I learned

  • Working from an existing open-source robot design
  • Prototype exploration under supervision
  • Hands-on learning with small robotic systems

Claw Machine – Team Project

Team-based course project completed in Robotics I.

This was a team project carried out as part of the Robotics I course at the University of Tartu. I worked together with Yiğithan Keskin, Björn Johanson, and Karl Erik Tammaru on the project.

My contribution focused mainly on the claw-related mechanical side, including 3D design and prototyping for the gripping mechanism as part of the broader team effort.

What I learned
