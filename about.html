About Me

Robotics • Embedded Systems • Human–Robot Interaction

Who I Am

I am a Robotics & Bio-Engineering student at the University of Tartu, with a strong interest in companion robots, embedded systems, and human–robot interaction.

My academic work and hands-on learning involve robotics documentation, prototyping, electronics, and small-scale robotic systems explored under supervision.

I am especially interested in expressive micro-robots that can support learning, motivation, and interaction in educational environments.

Education

BSc in Science & Technology (Robotics & Bio-Engineering)
University of Tartu, Estonia

Current Focus

My current focus is on supervised thesis-related work involving robotics documentation, prototype exploration, and small companion-robot form factors.

My long-term goal is to contribute to portable, practical, and accessible companion-robot systems that students can learn from, use, and build upon.
