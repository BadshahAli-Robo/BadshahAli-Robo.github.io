Robotics, embedded systems, and supervised student project work.

Robotic Study Companion (RSC)

Thesis-related documentation contribution under supervision.

As part of my thesis work under the supervision of Matevž Zorec and Farnaz Baksh, I contributed to the documentation of RSC v4 / v4.1 by creating short assembly videos and supporting the presentation of build steps.

My contribution focused on documenting and communicating the assembly process clearly, within a broader platform developed under my supervisors.

Pia Prototype Exploration

Supervised prototype exploration based on an open-source robot design.

As part of my supervised thesis work, I explored a prototype direction based on the open-source Pia the Robot design, focusing on form-factor exploration and hands-on learning.

This work builds upon the original open-source Pia design by pohukai (Printables model #190775), using its CAD files and design references as a foundation within a guided learning context.
